## Our approach to attribution

Attribution is not intended as a barrier to reuse.

Rabbit Hole Paradox wants its ideas to travel, evolve, be translated, remixed,
criticized, transformed, and incorporated into new works.

We ask for attribution because we believe ideas benefit from having a visible
history and a path back to their sources.

We do not expect attribution to become a burden.

If the format or circumstances make perfect attribution impractical, preserving
the ability of the work to circulate is more important to us than elaborate
crediting.
